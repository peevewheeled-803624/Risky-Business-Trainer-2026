Gameplay combines point-and-click progression, arcade-style minigames, unlockable animated scenes, upgrade perks, achievements, and a collectible gallery.

## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Risky Business**. This trainer enhances Risky Business by simplifying its clicker progression and timed minigames. It can provide extra points, reduce repetitive grinding, protect progress during challenges, and unlock episode or gallery content for players who want to focus on the scenes and story.

**Target Audience:** For players who want to reduce clicker grinding, make timed minigames easier, revisit gallery content quickly, or explore Risky Business without completing every progression requirement manually.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Risky Business is a point-and-click experience and does not include character movement or an explorable 3D environment. Use the mouse and keyboard to select menu options, advance dialogue, interact with prompts, and play its minigames.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional inventory system. Points, perks, upgrades, episode progress, achievements, and unlocked gallery scenes are managed through the game interface. Activate currency or upgrade options only after the relevant menu has been opened.

### World & Environment

Progress is organized through episodes and seasons rather than a free-roaming world. Complete or compatibility clicker interactions and minigames to advance, then use the gallery to replay unlocked scenes. The trainer is intended to support this structure without adding unrelated gameplay systems.

### Combat & Weapons

Risky Business has no conventional combat system. Its interactive challenges are clicker sequences and minigames, including timed inputs and selection-based tasks. Use the timer and no-fail options during these challenges when you want a more relaxed experience.
